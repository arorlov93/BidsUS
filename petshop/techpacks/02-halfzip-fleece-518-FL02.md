# Style 518-FL02 — Half-Zip Fleece with Crest ("Half-Zip 02") · v1 DRAFT

Category: midlayer · Sizes XS–L · Reference level: petshop/wa/ half-zip fleeces with crest.

## Description

2-leg (front legs only) half-zip pullover in heavy loopback fleece. Stand collar, chest-length
zipper, **embroidered 518 crest** on left side of back panel. Ribbed hem and cuffs. Quiet,
sport-classic look — no hood.

## Materials

| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| Body | Cotton/poly loopback fleece (French terry, brushed inside), 320–400 g/m², custom dye |
| Rib | 2×2 rib, matching body color, 5% spandex |
| Zipper | YKK #3 coil, 8–10 cm (size-graded), stand collar with inner zip garage at chin |
| Puller | Custom: matte metal or Paprika enamel drop |
| Embroidery | 518 crest, max 45 mm (XS/S) / 55 mm (M/L), flat stitch, backed |

## Construction

- Stand collar 4–5 cm, self-fabric outside / rib inside.
- Half-zip ends mid-chest; inner placket full length of zip.
- Raglan or set-in sleeves — pattern maker's call at R1, high armhole required.
- Rear hem angled up (short back), rib hem 2.5 cm.
- Sleeve rib cuffs 2 cm, elbow length.
- Coverstitch hems; no belly cutout needed (2-leg, chest panel ends before undercarriage).
- Comfort standard §2a applies: neck seam covered with soft tape, zip garage shields chin,
  underarm stretch tolerance checked at R1 on leggy AND stocky builds; weight S ≤ 90 g.
