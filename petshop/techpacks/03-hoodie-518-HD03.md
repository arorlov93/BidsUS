# Style 518-HD03 — Heavyweight Hoodie ("Hoodie 03") · v1 DRAFT

Category: midlayer · Sizes XS–L.

## Description

2-leg pullover hoodie in heavyweight fleece. Relaxed hood that stays flat when down, leash
opening at back neck, ribbed hem/cuffs, small woven flag label at hem. No drawcords (chew
safety), no kangaroo pocket, no prints.

## Materials

| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| Body | Cotton/poly brushed-back fleece 320+ g/m², custom dye, enzyme-washed hand |
| Rib | 2×2 rib, 5% spandex |
| Hood lining | Self-fabric, double layer |
| Woven flag label | "518" mini flag 20×10 mm at back hem, side inserted |

## Construction

- Hood: 2-panel, no drawcord, opening faced with self-fabric binding.
- **Leash opening**: 3 cm bound slit at center back 4 cm behind hood seam, flap cover —
  usable with harness under the hoodie.
- Short back, angled rear hem; rib hem 2.5 cm, elbow-length sleeves with 2 cm rib cuffs.
- High armholes; coverstitched seams; bar tacks at leash slit ends.
