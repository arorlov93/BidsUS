## Description

Fully-fashioned 2-leg pullover sweater, plain jersey or fine cable body (two variants to
sample), high ribbed turtleneck (fold-over), ribbed cuffs and hem. Quiet luxury hand-feel —
this is the "gift" SKU.

## Construction

- Fully fashioned: linked seams, no overlock on knit edges.
- Fold-over turtleneck; relaxed opening must stretch over head (≥ chest girth).
- Short back, angled rear hem; elbow-length sleeves.
- Anti-pilling finish; garment washed and blocked; loose yarn ends secured inside.
- Woven label sewn (not tagged) at inside back neck.
