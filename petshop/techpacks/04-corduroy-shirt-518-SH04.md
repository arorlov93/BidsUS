# Style 518-SH04 — Corduroy Shirt ("Shirt 04") · v1 DRAFT

Category: light layer / style piece · Sizes XS–L · Reference level: petshop/wa/ corduroy shirt.

## Description

2-leg button-front shirt in mid-wale corduroy. Classic shirt collar, front snap closure
(hidden functional snaps under buttons or snap-only — see Construction), curved short-back
hem. Menswear look scaled to small breeds.

## Materials

| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| Body | 100% cotton corduroy, 8–11 wale, 220–260 g/m², custom dye, softened finish |
| Closure | Sew-on buttons 11.5 mm (matte, tone-in-tone) **with hidden snaps underneath** — buttons decorative-secured (chew safety), snaps carry the load |
| Collar | One-piece shirt collar with light fusible |
| Label | Woven loop label at back neck |

## Construction

- Front opening full length (dressing ease for the owner) — 4–5 snaps size-graded.
- Buttons X-tacked with reinforced thread AND backed by snap — a button must never be the
  only closure (swallowing risk if bitten off).
- Collar sits flat; no chest pocket in v1.
- Curved hem, rear cut higher; side vents 1.5 cm.
- Elbow-length sleeves with clean 1 cm hem (no cuff).
- Flat-felled or safety-stitched seams; corduroy pile direction matched down the back.
