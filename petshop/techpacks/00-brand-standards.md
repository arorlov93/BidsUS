## 2. Small-breed fit principles (apply to every style)

1. **Short back**: garment back length ≤ body back length − 1 cm; rear hem angled up so hind
   legs and tail are fully free.
2. **High armholes**, narrow chest panel — front legs of toy breeds are close-set.
3. **Belly cutout** on all 4-leg garments and onesies: undercarriage hem cut back ("boy cut")
   with min. 6 cm clearance from rear edge, bound edge.
4. **Leash opening** on all outerwear and hoodie: 3 cm bound slit at center back, 4–6 cm
   behind collar seam, with fabric flap cover (snap or sewn top edge).
5. **Head-through openings** (pullover styles): neck rib must stretch to ≥ chest girth.
6. No hardware under the throat or in armpit contact zones. Total garment weight targets per
   style — a 2 kg dog must not carry a heavy garment.

- Hang tag: 60×90 mm, Bone uncoated card, Espresso print, cotton cord. Artwork supplied.

## 5. Packaging

1 pc / self-seal polybag with suffocation warning print, size sticker on bag (style / size /
color / barcode EAN-13 — codes supplied). Master carton ≤ 15 kg, export quality, no brand
advertising on carton. Fold standard: photo card supplied at PP-sample stage.

## 6. Quality, compliance, tolerances

- POM tolerance: ±1 cm on lengths/girths ≤ 60 cm; rib/stretch openings −0/+1.5 cm.
- Fabric: OEKO-TEX Standard 100 preferred; test reports (fiber content, colorfastness to
  rubbing wet/dry ≥ 3–4, saliva-safe dyes) with bulk.
- No small detachable parts that can be chewed off; all buttons/snaps functional-tested;
  embroidery backs covered (no scratchy stabilizer on skin side).
- Metal detection pass for needles. Seam strength: no seam failure at 8 kg pull on outerwear
  closures.
- AQL 2.5 final inspection (third party, buyer-appointed) before shipment.
