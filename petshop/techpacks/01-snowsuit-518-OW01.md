# Style 518-OW01 — HERO: 4-Leg Padded Snowsuit ("Snowsuit 01") · v1 DRAFT

Category: outerwear, winter · Sizes XS–L · Reference level: petshop/wa/ puffer suits
(Korean-style fit). This is the hero style — highest fit standard, 3 sample rounds minimum.

## Description

Full-coverage 4-leg padded snowsuit with hood. High funnel collar, back-entry zipper, elastic
leg cuffs, belly cutout, leash opening. Optional decorative ears on hood (separate SKU
variant — quote as add-on). Clean quilting, no prints.

## Materials

| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| Shell | Polyester pongee 380T, cire/matte finish, W/R coating (spray rating ≥ 3), custom dye |
| Insulation | Polyfill 200 g/m² body / 120 g/m² hood & legs. ALT: down 90/10 — quote both |
| Lining | Polyester taffeta 190T, Bone or tone-in-tone |
| Quilting | Horizontal channels 5 cm body; hood and legs unquilted or 8 cm |
| Zipper | YKK #3 coil, back entry, top-stop guard; matte tape tone-in-tone; Paprika or matte-metal puller |
| Leg cuffs | Soft elastic binding, non-marking, lycra binding 2 cm |
| Collar | Funnel, self-fabric + insulation, inner brushed tricot facing (chin comfort) |

## Construction

- Back-entry zip from collar to tail hem with full inner placket (no fur/zip contact).
- Hood: 3-panel, slightly oversized, sits flat when down; NOT removable in v1. Ears option:
  sewn-in padded ears on hood seam (variant B).
- Leash opening per brand standards §2.4 (slit + flap behind collar).
- Belly cutout per §2.3, bound with lycra binding.
- Rear hem angled, elasticized last 8 cm around haunches.
- Bar tacks at all stress points (leg/body joins, zip ends).
